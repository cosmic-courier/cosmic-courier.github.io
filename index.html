The Cosmic Courier

Space and science stories, told with wonder and checked against their sources.

The Cosmic Courier is an educational YouTube channel. Each video is an original explanation of recent space and science news, built from several openly licensed sources, with every source, author and image credited in the description. A Spanish-language sister channel, El Correo Cósmico, tells the same stories natively in Spanish.

About the publishing tool

The channel uses a private publishing tool, operated only by the channel owner, that uploads the channel's own videos and captions to YouTube as private drafts and prepares replies to viewer comments for the owner to approve. It connects to YouTube through Google sign-in. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
