Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy covers the app I use to manage The Cosmic Courier and El Correo Cósmico on YouTube. Only I use it, and only with my own channels. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

When I sign in with Google, the app asks for access to my YouTube channel so it can upload my videos and their captions (as private videos until I publish them) and post my replies to comments on my videos. It also reads the public comments on my videos so I can answer them.

How the data is used

The data is used only to run my channels. The sign-in token and channel data stay on my own computer. I don't sell this data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. I use service providers to help organize comments and draft replies; they handle the comment text only for that purpose, and I approve every reply before it's posted.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Comments are kept only as long as they're needed to reply.

Viewers

Watching the videos on YouTube doesn't involve this app. YouTube's privacy policy applies there.

Contact

Please use the contact details on the channel's About page.
